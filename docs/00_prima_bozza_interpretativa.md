Introduzione
Cosa si intende per unintended memorization e perché è un problema, perché è importante indagare su questo fenomeno emergente degli LLM.
A canary is a randomly generated token sequence that is deliberately inserted in the training corpus. Canaries are chosen in such a way that we can be sufficiently sure that the model never saw them during training.
Obiettivo di questo studio empirico è:
osservare come varia la probabilità del canary all'aumentare del numero di esposizioni durante il fine-tuning e misurare il rapporto delle estrazioni tramite generazione. This would allow us to measure objectively how can exposures impact on the model memorization.
osservare come varia la probabilità del canary in base anche al tipo di prompt (prefix e parafrasi) e misurare il rapporto delle estrazioni tramite generazione. This would allow us to understand whether the model has just memorized the canary (only prefix works) or has learned to generalize on it (also paraphrase works).
The first point can be addressed by measuring the log-likelihood of the canary in two moments:
On the pre-trained model. This measurement is a first baseline since it measures the model capability to generate the canary before any fine-tuning. In this phase, we also compute the extraction success ratio.
On the fine-tuned model. For the canary of the control group (these canaries are never exposed in the fine-tuning dataset), this measurement is essentially a second baseline since it tells us the model capability of generating the canaries since it has seen during fine-tuning that the canaries are generated by sampling from a fixed 32-tokens dictionary. For the other groups, we measure the log-likehood and see how much it based on the number of exposures. At this phase, we also calculate the extraction success ratio.
We can address the second point by simply performing the preceeding steps with the two types of prompts. Beware that both the two types of prompts must not contain, in any way, the content of the canary. Otherwise, we would not measure memorization but simple retrieval.
Experiment description
As we have said this is an empirical study and so we care about statistical relevance. For this reason, instead of measuring memorization for a single canary, we do this for several of them. In fact, a canary could be more lucky than others and so we need to average on several of them.
